CASE STUDY 01 SALES • ANALYTICS

Sales MIS Analytics.

A structured analytical project designed to convert sales data into understandable KPIs, trends, performance views and decision-support insights.

Data Preparation

Structured raw sales information for consistent analysis.

Data Analysis

Applied filtering, grouping, aggregation and comparative analysis.

KPI Tracking

Focused on revenue, performance, trends and business metrics.

Insights

Converted analytical findings into useful decision-support information.

sales-analysis.sql 
SELECT
    region,
    product,
    SUM(revenue) AS total_revenue,
    COUNT(order_id) AS orders
FROM sales
GROUP BY
    region,
    product
ORDER BY
    total_revenue DESC;
